02

A short lesson in motion for the moving picture

Animation Issue No. 02

Motion prompts for Runway, Kling, Pika, Luma, and similar tools. You do not need the jargon. The colour in each sentence shows which part is which.

Runway Kling Pika Luma Sora-like

Prompt anatomy

subject + motion + camera + style + timing

A quiet cedar forest stands beside a still lake. Thin clouds drift and the grass tips sway. The camera slowly pushes in, easing closer as if to notice a detail. The footage looks like a cinematic film at 24 frames per second, with fine grain and natural motion. What moves in the scene does so gently. The shot lasts 5 seconds. The clip plays once, with a clear beginning and end.

  • Subject — what the picture is of.
  • Motion — what moves inside the scene.
  • Camera — how the frame itself travels.
  • Style — the film look.
  • Timing — pace, length, and whether it loops.
